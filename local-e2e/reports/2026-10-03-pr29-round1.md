# PR #29 round 1: durable Income acknowledgements and warm-up retry

## Summary and design

- `2e4980c` removes the alpha Income early-success path. The production runtime now uses the tested resolver for
  every transfer kind: full matched Hub credit permits ACK publication, but durable work remains until the Spoke
  Vault confirms ArrivalConfirmed or RefundRecognized. Failed publication, lost delivery and restart retain retries.
- The harness queues every transit in reports, including Income. ACK execution alone, ExpiryAttested, and a passed
  deadline no longer complete durable work; completion requires a confirmed Spoke Vault terminal state.
- `d268fcf` derives Ana's whole shares and actual charge from the mined deposit price, checks the remainder retained
  in her wallet and reconciles Idle. Management fee accrual during default warm-up no longer breaks the assertion.
- Default warm-up snapshots/restores the durable keeper queue with the chain snapshots. Otherwise completed warm-up
  transit IDs suppress ACK retries when the reverted chain replays the same sends.
