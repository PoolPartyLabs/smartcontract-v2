### 3.2 Every division and rounding

VRR has no division and no multiplication; the only arithmetic is `_age` (VRR:280, clamped at 0, favours the reporter by treating a future timestamp as age 0), `block.timestamp + maxReportAge` (VRR:179, checked) and `indexPlusOne - 1` (VRR:154, cannot underflow after VRR:153).

Defensive reading (corrected in review): the price source always floors, but the fund total is **not** floored in one direction. Added legs (CVL:202, 242, 246) round the total down and the subtracted unknown-origin term (CVL:281-283, CVL:188) rounds it up, so at a mint the rounding is on the actor's side by at most `sum over added priced legs of (1 + amount / 1e18)` USDC base units (for a 1,000 WETH leg, about 1,001 base units, 0.001 USDC) and at a payout on the actor's side by at most `sum over subtracted priced terms of (1 + amount / 1e18)`. Both bounds are dust at MVP amounts, but a claim "rounding always favours the fund" is false as stated; INV-REPORT-41 pins the signed bound. A mode-dependent ceiling belongs to the consumer (CVL:316); it is recorded as an open point for the core card (Q7), not a defect here.

### 3.3 Monotonic counters and the state machine

- Per spoke `i`: `lastWormholeSequence` and `lastReportSequence` strictly increase on every accepted delivery **after the first** (VRR:160-162, VRR:172-174, written VRR:183-188) and never change otherwise; the first acceptance writes arbitrary values, including `(0, 0)` over the default `(0, 0)` (`test_DEC093_firstReportAcceptsSequenceZeroAndNotifiesCoreVault`), so "strictly increase" is false for the `None -> Accepted` step (corrected in review, also in INV-REPORT-04); `acceptedAt` equals the hub time of the last acceptance (non-decreasing under A-TIME); `reportTimestamp` is **not** forced to increase by the receiver (only by A-SPOKE: the emitter stamps `block.timestamp` of its own chain).
- States of spoke `i`: `None` (`acceptedAt == 0`) and `Accepted(w, r, ts, at)`. Transitions: `None -> Accepted` by any delivery passing the checks (the first report accepts any sequence, VRR:142-143, VRR:157); `Accepted(w, r) -> Accepted(w', r')` iff `w' > w`, `r' > r` and the checks pass. No transition back to `None`; an acceptance at `block.timestamp == 0` leaves the spoke in `None` (CF-R5). Derived, time-dependent predicate: `Fresh` while `max(0, now - ts) <= maxReportAge` (VRR:244-249).
- Reachable-state predicate (added in review; it is `WF-VRR` of `03-FORMAL-VERIFICATION-PREPARATION.md` section 5.2, and every step proof assumes it): under A-TIME (`1 <= now < 2^64`), `acceptedAt_i == 0` implies all four fields are 0 and `_payload[i]` is empty; `acceptedAt_i != 0` implies `acceptedAt_i <= now`, `ts_i <= acceptedAt_i + maxAge_i`, and `_payload[i]` decodes to a report whose `sequence == lastR_i` and `timestamp == ts_i`. The sentinel also collapses at `now == k * 2^64` through the cast at VRR:187 (unreachable, excluded by the same assumption).
- CLPS has no state machine: every token is fixed in one of three kinds at construction.
- RC constants shared with the producer: `ARRIVAL_WINDOW` equals `SpokeVaultTypes.ARRIVAL_WINDOW` (pinned by `test/unit/spoke/SpokeVaultSpoke.t.sol:571`); `VERSION = 2` is compiled into both sides from the same library.

## 9. Formal verification readiness

### 9.1 Strategy: a scalar core, two lemmas, and induction for sequences

`deliver` decomposes into five parts, each with its own tool: (a) the Core Bridge answer, summarised by assumption A-WH1; (b) ABI decoding of the payload, compiler-generated and element-uniform, isolated by lemma **L-SHAPE** (INV-REPORT-18: nothing after VRR:164 branches on array content); (c) the **scalar decision**, a function of at most 13 words (the packed `_state[i]` word, five VM fields, four report scalars, `now`, the spoke's `chainId` and `maxReportAge`) using only comparisons, one checked addition (VRR:179) and one clamped subtraction (VRR:280); (d) the writes, whose frame is a static fact (INV-REPORT-05, Slither writer sets); (e) the callback, modelled as arbitrary code that may revert or re-enter, handled by the guard and by EVM revert semantics (INV-REPORT-07). Part (c) has no loop and no nonlinear arithmetic, so a Halmos or hevm PASS over symbolic scalars with one fixed payload shape already covers every value of those scalars; Kontrol makes it a bytecode proof, and L-SHAPE lifts the shape.

Properties over sequences close by induction: INV-REPORT-04 (each step from an accepted state strictly increases `lastW_i`, `lastR_i` or leaves them untouched; the `None -> Accepted` step is the base case, not a step of the increase) plus 07 (failed calls write nothing, an EVM fact made reliable by the V0 gate) plus 05 (no other writer) plus 33 (`now >= 1`) give INV-REPORT-03 for every sequence, because once `(w, r)` is accepted `lastW_i >= w` forever, so redelivering `w` fails at VRR:160. INV-REPORT-32 needs only two steps: after `deliver(v1)`, `lastW_i = w1 < w2` and `lastR_i = r1 < r2`, and the other checks of `v2` do not read `_state`. The price source is stateless, so every property is one call.
