3. Seven of the 29 internal library functions have no caller in `src/` since the 2026-09-29 fee ruling (IA `advanceSource`, `recognizeFromSource`, `isSourceFlagged`, `incomeTokens`, 3-argument `takeOwed`; SM `isWholeShares`; MD `bridgeAdapterFor`, whose logic is re-implemented at CVL:692-705). 13 of the 31 IA test functions and the IA invariant handler exercise that dead path; the production path is `distribute` + `checkpoint` + `takeOwed` only.

## 2. External surface

No library function moves a token or makes an external call; IA emits its events in the caller's context. Reentrancy protection is the caller's (`nonReentrant` on every listed entry).

Review addition, fee base (not a division site of its own): `previewDeposit` takes the flow fee on the offered `usdcAmount` (SM:122), while only `usdcForShares + fee` leaves the wallet (CV:77, CV:84-86); the remainder below one share's price pays the fee without entering the fund. Measured effective rate on the amount that entered: 198 bps at the 100 bps cap (probe R2: 1.98 USDC offered at 1.00 per share, 1 share, fee 0.0198 USDC); bound `fee * (1e4 - bps) < bps * (usdcForShares + 1 + ceil(p / 1e18))`, so at most about twice the nominal rate (INV-MATH-53, MM-8).
