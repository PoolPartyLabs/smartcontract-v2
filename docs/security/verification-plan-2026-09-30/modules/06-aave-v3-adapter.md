**What this card changes in the plan** (read first):

1. **Two exit defects reproduced today, both with a one-line remedy** (section 4.1): F1, foreign aTokens in the adapter turn a deferred income withdrawal into a `LedgerUnderflow` revert of the whole exit, so a close the reserve can pay in full reverts (P9: 1,000 USDC principal, 100 USDC income, 1,110 USDC of reserve liquidity, 500 USDC of donated aTokens: `closePosition` reverts; the same close without the donation pays 1,000 + 100). This defeats the AAVE-4 fix. F2, the full-exit path accepts a short `withdraw(max)` and closes the position silently (P1), against the adapter's own rule at AA:104. Both get `FLAGGED` pins (W2) before any fuzzing.
2. **The spoke card's assumption A-SPK-1 on adapters does not hold for this adapter** (`02-spoke-vault.md:399`; the earlier citation `:358` is stale): `cumulativeIncome` is only monotonic up to the rounding of two Aave calls (pinned at UF:105-128), and exits can revert on F1. Already propagated: INV-SPOKE-25 is in epsilon form (`02-spoke-vault.md:278`) and INV-SPOKE-30 carries the F1 caveat (`:283`).
3. **This is the most tractable value-moving contract for formal work**: no linked library, no DELEGATECALL, no assembly and no `unchecked` of its own, one loop bounded by the constructor list, and 14,443 bytes of runtime margin. The obstacle is nonlinear `Math.mulDiv` against a symbolic index and the Aave model. Tier targets follow `03-FORMAL-VERIFICATION-PREPARATION.md:100` (corrected by the review, section 11): of the 51 properties of section 6, 22 are V3 or V4 targets (17 class A or B, 5 class C through lemmas L0, L0c, L7, L9), 14 are bounded V2 targets (INV-AAVE-09 and -28 carried as `cover_` until the F1 and F2 rulings; -48 to -50 added by the review), 1 is V0 (opcode scan); the other 14 are multi-transaction or gas properties at V1 (6, INV-AAVE-23 with its F1 `cover_`), fork (2) or unit (6).

## 4. Threat model (defensive)

Actors assumed hostile: the manager (drives every vault verb with any argument the vault forwards), any third party (donations of the asset to the adapter or to the aToken, aToken transfers in, `supply` on behalf of the adapter, borrowing the reserve dry, front-running), the guardian key (compromised), and every dependency failing (Aave Pool paused, frozen, illiquid, upgraded to misbehave; asset paused or blacklisting). The adapter's own code is trusted only because the vault pins its codehash (SV:192, :694-699) and the runtime has no opcode that could change it.

### 4.1 Findings the threat model raises (my severity reading, for the coordinator and the founder; none is an auditor's verdict)

Each finding gets a `FLAGGED` pin in W2 that asserts today's behaviour and flips when a ruling changes the code.

- **F1, major candidate (DEC-056, DEC-080, AAVE-4): foreign aTokens turn a deferred income withdrawal into a revert of the whole exit.** After the principal leaves (AA:423-424), Aave's burn rounding can leave the ledger worth 1 or 2 units less than `inc`; `_takeIncome` still asks `min(inc, balanceOf(aToken))` (AA:444). Without foreign units Aave refuses (the amount exceeds the adapter's balance), the `catch` pays 0 and the income stays pending. With foreign units Aave pays, burns more scaled units than `s`, and `LedgerUnderflow` (AA:494) reverts the call. Reproduced (P9 re-run by the review): P9 (close, index 1.1, 1,000 principal, 100 income, 500 donated, liquidity 1,110: reverts; no donation, where the reserve then holds only 1,100.000002 USDC so no borrower is needed: pays 1,000 + 100); P7 (decrease leaving 1 unit of principal, index 1.011 to 2.454 ray: 8 of 120 reverts with a 10 USDC supply on behalf, 0 of 120 without); P8 (1,000,000 USDC position, index about 1.05 to 1.29 ray: 4 of 240). Reach: foreign units worth a few base units make the decrease case possible (the gap is Aave's burn rounding, a few base units; the probes used 10 USDC); the close case needs reserve liquidity in `[V, V + foreign value)`, which happens naturally near 100% utilization (`docs/REVIEW-LOG-2026-09-29.md:46`) or can be engineered by a borrower. Mitigation today: the Core Vault pays from Idle when the unwind reverts (CV:204-214); a decrease leaving 2 or more units works; the manager can clear it by `collectIncome` then `closePosition` (probe Q6: the close reverts, collect pays 100e6, the close then succeeds and the key leaves, with 500e6 foreign scaled units left in the adapter at S1). The automatic unwind cannot use that sequence (SV:859-862), so payouts stay exposed. Remedy proposal: clamp `take` to `_value(l, index)` at AA:444 (C1, section 9.6).
- **F2, minor (DEC-068): the full exit accepts a short payment silently.** `withdraw(asset, max, vault)` (AA:399) is never compared with the ledger value or the pre-call aToken balance; a short `withdrawn` is split income first and principal short (AA:405-410), the ledger is zeroed and the key closed. P1 (pool paying 1 unit less): `principal0 = 999,999,999`, `income0 = 100,000,000`, key closed, no revert; P2 the same through `decreasePosition(max)`. This contradicts "never accept a silent partial withdrawal" (AA:104), which `_withdraw` enforces (AA:491). Needs a non-conforming pool (AS2), hence minor; it also hits the zero-hit branch AA:407-408. Remedy: C2.
- **F3, open ruling (DEC-068 against DEC-069): `unwindExitParams` sizes by principal only (AA:340-350).** The hub unwind has no try/catch per step (SV:506-508), so when the reserve holds less than the part asked, the whole unwind reverts and the payout falls back to Idle (CV:204-214), although some liquidity was available. DEC-068 says "pay what is possible"; DEC-069 says "an illiquid position is waited on". Capping the part at available liquidity would change the IAdapter contract ("at least", IAD:173-178). P5 also shows that a key holding only income returns `(true, "")` (the vault skips it at SV:858).
- **F4, minor (INV-CONS-5 of the corpus): swallowed Aave failures leave no event.** Both `catch` blocks (AA:413-416, :485-487) proceed silently; on-chain, deferred income is visible only through a later `positionValue`. The operation still emits its verb event (process rule), but not why income was 0. Remedy: C3.
- **F5, minor, Not verified: the sufficiency bound is the aToken's asset balance (AA:444), not Aave's available liquidity.** IAP:53-54 already names two failure causes (virtual balance and aToken balance). When the two diverge (a direct asset transfer to the aToken raises the aToken balance but not the virtual balance), the best-effort withdrawal reverts and pays 0 instead of the available part. Income liveness only.
- **F6, minor, pinned (Q60): `cumulativeIncome` regresses by Aave rounding** in two paths (UF:97-128) and its formula differs from IAD:196-197 and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:170-171` (acknowledged at AA:311-316). Consequence outside this module: the spoke card's A-SPK-1 and INV-SPOKE-25 (see "What this card changes", item 2).
- **F7, low: the constructor trusts the positional decode of the legacy `getReserveData` layout** (AA:133, IAP:7-10) without cross-checking the aToken (for example its underlying asset). A future Pool upgrade that changes the layout would wire a wrong aToken into newly created funds only; fork tests re-pinned per release catch it (W11).
- **F8, documentation drift:** `docs/REVIEW-LOG-2026-09-29.md:37` (AAVE-1) and `:41` (AAVE-5) describe the pre-final-verification behaviour (whole income on every exit; increase reverts without liquidity); `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md:129` records the fix. The card uses the code, not those readings.

Added by the adversarial review (section 11). Each needs a failing or non-conforming dependency, which the defensive posture assumes; each gets a `FLAGGED` pin in W2 and an H mode of the pool model (section 9.5).
- **F9, major candidate if AS3 or AS4 fails (DEC-080, DEC-068): silent write-off of the whole position.** `_exit` zeroes `s` and `P` (AA:395-396) and returns `(0, true)` when `scaledBalanceOf(adapter)` answers 0 (AA:397-398), without checking that the ledger held nothing. Probe Q2 (1,000 principal, 100 income): `closePosition` returns zero amounts, emits `PositionClosed`, the key leaves, 1,000e6 scaled units stay in the adapter unreachable until a re-open, and the vault drops the position (SV:763-767), so the hub's Share Assets fall by the principal with no revert. Remedy C7: return early only when `ledgerScaled == 0`, else revert `LedgerUnderflow(ledgerScaled, heldScaled)`.
- **F10, minor under AS2 (DEC-068, DEC-080, AA:104): reports rest on Aave's return value alone.** Full path: `attributable` derives from `withdrawn` (AA:399-401) with no check of the burn or of what reached the vault (Q5a: reported 1,000 + 100, received 0, key closed). Remedy C8: require `burned >= floor(amount * 1e27 / I)` on the exact path and `heldScaled - scaledBalanceOf(adapter) >= ledgerScaled` on the full path, or measure the vault's asset balance before and after (a check, never a value base, DEC-080).
- **F11, minor under AS1 (DEC-067, DEC-068): an index below the last measured one is accepted.** `lastIndex` is written on every verb and never read (AA:52-54); Q4 lowers the index from 1.1e27 to 1.0e27 and `positionValue.principal0` falls from 999,999,999 to 909,090,909 while `lastIndex` stays 1.1e27. The hub prices deposits from it without `try` (CVL:224-225, :246). Remedy C9: in the views and verbs, revert when `index < l.lastIndex` or `index < 1e27` (fail closed: deposits stop, PAYOUT mode uses the last value, CVL:227-230; exits of an open key would also stop, which the founder must weigh against paying a principal computed at a garbage index).
- **F12, design question (DEC-069, DEC-068, INV-EXIT-2 of the corpus): the manager can make liquid value illiquid just before claims.** No entry verb is refused for low reserve liquidity (AA:170-179, :196-205); with utilization near 100% (`docs/REVIEW-LOG-2026-09-29.md:46`), USDC supplied minutes before a claim can be borrowed at once, and the claim's unwind then reverts (row 4(a)) and pays partially (CV:204-214). No defence in this module; possible rules for the founder: an entry-side liquidity floor in the adapter, or a spoke rule that blocks entries while payout requests are open.
- **Checked and not a finding: EIP-150 starvation of the `try` branches** (threat class 11, probe Q3).

### 9.4 Obstacles in the current code

- Nonlinear arithmetic: `Math.mulDiv` at AA:347, :401, :501 (512-bit, OpenZeppelin assembly), and Aave's own `floor`/`ceil` divisions in the model. Answered by the W9 lemmas and concrete index sets.
- External calls: 18 call sites in the runtime (section 2 lists them). All go to three contracts (Pool, aToken, asset), so one model contract can play Pool and aToken if `getReserveData` returns the model's own address as `aTokenAddress`.
- `try`/`catch` (AA:399-416, :482-487): both branches need a model flag; a Halmos or hevm run that never sets it proves nothing about the fallback.
- Data-dependent loops: only over `_assets` (AA:129-137, :327-334), fixed at construction; bound 2.
- Mapping of structs with a packed word (`aToken` and `open` share word 0 of each `Ledger`): writing symbolic ledgers with `vm.store` is error-prone; reach states through calls instead, or use Kontrol `symbolicStorage` with explicit structural preconditions.
- `abi.decode` of `bytes calldata` (AA:232, :461): symbolic lengths are unsupported in Halmos (`01-TOOLING.md` section 5.8); fix the lengths.
- One `MCOPY` in the runtime (source construct Not verified; Halmos needs its size concrete, `01-TOOLING.md` section 5.8).
- Immutables in the runtime (`vault`, `pool`, `guardian`): Kontrol needs a concrete deployment or `--symbolic-immutables`.
- Constructor external call (AA:133): Halmos requires a single-path constructor, so the model returns a concrete aToken.
- Added by the review, from `03-FORMAL-VERIFICATION-PREPARATION.md`: the OpenZeppelin 5.7.0 guard slot `0x9b77...5f00` must be constrained `!= 2` (`ENTERED`, `ReentrancyGuard.sol:51`) in every symbolic-storage route, or every guarded verb reverts on every path and the proof is vacuous (section 4.3 there, WF-AAVE in section 5.2); INV-AAVE-07 is a hyperproperty and runs on twin instances deployed in `setUp`, never with a snapshot cheatcode (section 4.3); INV-AAVE-04 needs a recording token that logs every transfer destination (section 4.3); the H modes of F9 to F11 are model switches, each with a `cover_` for both values (section 8.2 item V6).
- Not obstacles here: no DELEGATECALL, no linked library, no CREATE or clone, no report decoding, no Q128 index, no own assembly.
